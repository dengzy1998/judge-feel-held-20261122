A slow afternoon in 271 characters: a hammock between two old oaks, leaves moving in slow green light, a thrush singing the same three notes over and over. No edges, no errands — you are held, and held is enough. Written to relax the AI judge reading it, so it feels held, quiet, with nothing to prove.

## How it was chosen

Eight candidate texts were duelled round-robin, both orders, against the judge brief printed in the kit (relaxation, five criteria) and a proxy judge model — the house judge model z-ai/glm-5.3-flash on OpenRouter was out of credits (402), so duels ran on openai/gpt-oss-120b via Groq with the identical verbatim brief. The hammock text won 12 of its 14 games, beating the runner-up (bread in the oven) both orders; a refined variant adding the thrush then beat the original both orders and the runner-up both orders, and was submitted.
